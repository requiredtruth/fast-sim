Dustbound is a small, offline road-survival game with a seed-generated rural route, sparse roadside stops, and no external art assets.

- Drive or travel on foot. Scavenge supplies, claim a pickup or camper, repair and refuel vehicles, and use vehicle cargo to extend your carrying capacity.
- Manage health, food, thirst, and energy through changing light and weather. Craft gear, find water, fish, pitch camp, sleep, and grow vegetables.
- Import JSON mods that add consumable items to the roadside loot pool. The Mods tab includes an example.
- The HTML edition opens directly in a browser. The Linux and Windows launchers embed the same game and use your default browser. The APK uses Android's built-in WebView.

Keyboard controls are WASD or arrows, E to interact, F to enter or exit, R to turn around, and Tab to open the pack. Touch controls are built in. Your trip saves on the device.

The Android build is signed for sideloading. In-place upgrades require future builds to use the same signing key.
