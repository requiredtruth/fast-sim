Long Road is an endless highway survival game, forked from Out on Parole.

- One road with no end, through desert, plains, forest, badlands and snowy mountains. It has day and night, rain, fog, snow and dust storms.
- Stops along the way: gas stations, diners, motels, farms, garages, ranger stations, trading posts, campgrounds, small towns and pileups. You can walk into the buildings and search them.
- Survive on food, water, rest and warmth. Bleeding and sickness, campfires, tents and sleeping.
- Your car runs on fuel and wears out its engine, tyres, battery and coolant. You can siphon, strip and claim abandoned cars, and keep your gear in the trunk.
- Over 60 items, crafting, traders, hunting, fishing and four weapons. Raider gangs and raider cars, and wolves at night.
- Text mods that add or change items and recipes and tune the game. The Mods screen includes an example.
- Every model, texture and animation is built from code.

Builds: Android APK (tilt to steer and drive, touch controls), a single-file browser version, Windows and Linux.
The APK is signed for sideloading.
