## Five worlds

- **Long Road** keeps the original endless trip through changing biomes.
- **Metro Grid** adds an original city with cross streets, marked crossings, pocket parks, storefronts, garages and a skyline.
- **Pine Country** brings dense woods, ranger stations and fishing ponds.
- **Frostline** starts in snow, with cold nights, garages and motels.
- **Red Mesa** has red-rock terrain, wrecks and trading posts.

Each world has its own save. Existing trips appear under **Long Road**.

## Items and fixes

- Drop supplies as 3D objects. Eighteen procedural item shapes use finishes for metal, glass, rubber and other surfaces. Ground caches survive saving and reopening.
- Find 22 additional foods, medicines, car parts, trade goods and crafting materials. Eight new recipes include fish stew, a canvas tent and a resin tyre patch.
- Fixed the pause shortcut, flashlight lighting away from camp, misplaced parked cars, world flags leaking between trips, and depleted containers refilling after streaming or loading.
- Android installers from v2.0.0 use a different signing certificate. Back up any trips and mods you want to keep before uninstalling v2.0.0 to install this APK.
- Android supports ARM64 and ARMv7 with native libraries aligned for 16 KB page devices.

Models and textures are generated by the game. Mods continue to use the existing text format.
